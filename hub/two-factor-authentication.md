# Two-Factor Authentication

Enable time-based one-time password (TOTP) multi-factor authentication from
**Settings → Security**. MFA adds a second proof at sign-in and protects an
account even when a password or connected identity is compromised.

Store recovery information in a secure place that is separate from the device
running the authenticator. Do not share one-time codes with support, collaborators,
or an application.

Organization policy can require stronger authentication for access to shared
resources. See [Account Security](/docs/hub/security) and
[Organization Single Sign-On](/docs/hub/organization-sso).
