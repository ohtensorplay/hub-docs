# Subscriptions and Plans

MEGA subscriptions unlock plan features for either an individual or an
organization. They are separate from prepaid compute credit, which pays for
metered Jobs, paid Space hardware, and routed inference.

The [Pricing page](/pricing) shows the current offers, prices, included
allowances, and regional availability. Check it before purchasing because plan
details can change.

## Choose the billing owner first

| If you need features for… | Choose… |
| --- | --- |
| Your own account | A personal plan |
| A shared workspace and its members | An organization plan |

A personal plan belongs only to your account. It does not grant features to
organizations you belong to. An organization plan belongs to that organization;
only its administrators can manage it.

## Available plans

| Plan | Owner | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Personal account or organization | Publish, collaborate, and use the standard Hub features. |
| [PRO](/docs/hub/pro-plan) | Personal account | Extra personal publishing, development, and capacity features. |
| [Team](/docs/hub/team-and-enterprise-plans#team) | Organization | Shared governance and collaboration controls. |
| [Enterprise](/docs/hub/team-and-enterprise-plans#enterprise) | Organization | Advanced identity, security, and commercial support needs. |

See the Pricing page for eligibility and the exact features included with each
plan.

## Subscribe

1. Sign in to the personal account or organization that should own the plan.
2. Open [Pricing](/pricing) and select the appropriate offer.
3. For an organization plan, choose the organization and confirm the number of
   seats before payment.
4. Complete the checkout and return to the relevant billing page to verify the
   plan status.

Enterprise plans use the contact option on the Pricing page rather than a
self-service purchase flow.

## Manage or change a plan

Open **Settings → Billing** for a personal subscription, or the organization's
**Settings → Billing** page for an organization subscription. The page shows
the plan, renewal information, and available management actions.

Plan changes, cancellation, payment updates, and invoices are managed for the
selected owner. Changing a personal subscription never changes an organization
subscription, and vice versa.

## Plan availability in the product

When a feature requires a plan, MEGA labels it in the relevant settings or
workflow. A disabled control can also result from your organization role, so
confirm both the subscription and your permissions. Organization capabilities
are described in [Organizations](/docs/hub/organizations).

For balances, metered workloads, invoices, and charge support, see
[Billing](/docs/hub/billing).
