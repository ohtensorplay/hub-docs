# Team and Enterprise Plans

Team and Enterprise are subscriptions for MEGA organizations. They unlock
organization capabilities; they do not change an individual member's personal
plan.

The [Pricing page](/pricing) is authoritative for current prices, seat rules,
included capacity, and commercial terms.

## Team

Team is for organizations that need shared governance alongside everyday Hub
work. The current Team offer includes organization features such as:

- organization token governance and OAuth applications;
- single sign-on, audit logs, and resource groups;
- organization inference billing and monthly routed-inference credit per seat;
- organization Gradio, static, and Docker Spaces; and
- higher organization capacity for Jobs, inference requests, Space builds, and
  Hub access.

An organization administrator can select Team from [Pricing](/pricing). Choose
the organization, confirm the required seats, complete checkout, and verify the
plan in the organization's **Settings → Billing** page.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is for organizations with advanced identity, network-security, and
commercial requirements. It includes Team capabilities and can add:

- organization-owned service accounts;
- SCIM provisioning and workload identity federation;
- trusted-network controls;
- longer audit-log retention; and
- a managed agreement with licensed seats and a fixed term.

Use the Enterprise contact option on [Pricing](/pricing) to discuss eligibility,
commercial terms, and onboarding.

## Roles and access

An organization plan makes eligible controls available, but it does not bypass
organization roles. Only administrators can manage the organization's billing,
and members need the relevant role to use a feature. Refer to
[Organizations](/docs/hub/organizations), [Organization Single Sign-On](/docs/hub/organization-sso),
and [Resource Groups](/docs/hub/resource-groups) for feature-specific setup.

## Manage the subscription

Open the organization's **Settings → Billing** page to review the plan,
licensed seats, renewal information, and available actions. Personal Billing
does not manage an organization subscription. See [Billing](/docs/hub/billing)
for payment and charge support.
