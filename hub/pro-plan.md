# PRO Plan

PRO is the subscription for an individual MEGA account. It adds personal
features and capacity; it does not apply to organizations you belong to.

See [Pricing](/pricing) for the current price, billing intervals, and complete
allowance list.

## What PRO includes

The current PRO offer can include:

- Gradio, static, and Docker Spaces for your personal account;
- Space development mode;
- monthly routed-inference credit;
- a PRO badge on your community profile; and
- higher personal capacity for Jobs, inference requests, Space builds, and Hub
  access.

The live Pricing page is authoritative for exact limits and availability.

## Subscribe

Sign in, open [Pricing](/pricing), and choose **Get PRO**. Complete checkout
for your personal account, then check **Settings → Billing** to confirm that
the plan is active.

## Use PRO features

Personal-only features are available under your account. For example, create a
Space in your personal namespace and select an available Space SDK or enable
development mode from its settings. See [Spaces](/docs/hub/spaces) for Space
setup.

PRO does not turn on organization features. To use paid capabilities in a
shared organization, an organization administrator must choose an appropriate
organization plan.

## Manage PRO

Open **Settings → Billing** to see renewal information and the available
actions for your subscription. For billing changes or charge support, see
[Billing](/docs/hub/billing).
