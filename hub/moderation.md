# Community Moderation

Repository discussions, papers, and community content can be reported when
they violate applicable policy or create a safety concern. Use the reporting
controls on the affected page and include only the information needed to
explain the issue.

Moderation outcomes may appear in the [Notifications](/docs/hub/notifications)
inbox. Do not publish private messages, credentials, or personal data in a
report. For routine collaboration, use
[Discussions and Pull Requests](/docs/hub/discussions).
