# Billing

Billing has two separate parts:

- A [subscription](/docs/hub/subscriptions-and-plans) provides plan features
  for a personal account or organization.
- Prepaid compute credit pays for metered workloads, including paid Space
  hardware, Jobs, and routed inference.

The [Pricing page](/pricing) is the source of truth for current plans, prices,
included allowances, and availability.

## Find the right billing page

Use **Settings → Billing** for a personal account. For an organization, open
that organization's **Settings → Billing** page. Personal billing and
organization billing are separate: a personal subscription does not change an
organization's plan or credit, and organization administrators manage the
organization's billing.

## Manage a subscription

From the relevant billing page, you can review the current plan, billing
period, renewal date, and subscription status. Use **Manage billing** to
update payment details, change the billing period, or cancel when those options
are available for the plan.

After a billing change, refresh the billing page before relying on a newly
available feature. If the displayed plan or status does not match the change,
contact support with the billing owner and the time of the change; do not share
payment details in a support request.

See [Subscriptions and Plans](/docs/hub/subscriptions-and-plans) for plan
ownership and eligibility, [PRO Plan](/docs/hub/pro-plan) for personal-plan
features, and [Team and Enterprise Plans](/docs/hub/team-and-enterprise-plans)
for organization plans.

## Prepaid compute credit

Compute credit belongs to the selected personal account or organization. Add
credit from that owner's billing page, then select the same owner when creating
or running work that supports organization billing.

You can review the balance and recent usage from the billing page. A paid
workload may be unavailable when its owner does not have enough credit; add
credit or select the intended billing owner before retrying.

For workload-specific behavior, see [Jobs](/docs/hub/jobs),
[Spaces](/docs/hub/spaces), and [Inference Providers](/docs/inference-providers/index).

## Get help with a charge

Use the billing page to identify the billing owner and the relevant
subscription or credit activity. Contact support for duplicate, unexpected, or
failed charges. Include the owner, approximate time, and the non-sensitive
reference shown in the billing page. Never include a card number, security
code, password, or access token.
