# Delete Your Account

Open **Settings → Account** to request account deletion. Review the affected
personal repositories, memberships, tokens, keys, subscriptions, and compute
credit before confirming the request.

If you own an organization, transfer administration or promote another
administrator first. Export any data you need to retain, revoke third-party
application access, and stop automation that uses your credentials.

Account deletion is a security-sensitive browser workflow. Do not attempt it
through a shared script or by giving another person your credentials.
