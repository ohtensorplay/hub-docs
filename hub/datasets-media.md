# Image, Audio, and Video Datasets

Media datasets need more context than a file list. State media format,
sampling or frame rate where relevant, annotations, split rules, consent or
rights basis, and known quality limits in the dataset card.

Avoid embedding personal or sensitive media in examples unless the distribution
rights and privacy review are clear. Use a private or
[Gated Repository](/docs/hub/gated-repositories) while access terms are being
reviewed.
